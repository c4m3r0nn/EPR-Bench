# Clinical decisions log

Every clinical judgement built into how EPR-Bench marks an episode, or into what the simulated patients and staff do,
in one table: what is implemented, why, who decided it, how confident we are, and where it lives. It is the single
list for clinical reviewers to work through.

Since 25 November 2026 the project's policy is that the developer (an AI coding agent working for the owner, a UK
doctor) makes an educated clinical guess where a marking detail is open, applies it and logs it here as
**AI-proposed**, rather than waiting for the clinical lead. Rulings the clinical lead has given are marked as such,
with the date and, where it was written down, the clinical lead's own words. Nothing here has had an independent
second clinical review. The cases are drafts on fictional patients.

## How to review

Read a row, and if you think it is wrong, open a GitHub issue at https://github.com/c4m3r0nn/EPR-Bench/issues with the
row's ID in the title (for example "D-A05: four hours is too short after a single dose"), quote the row, and say what
you would do instead and why. Severity and your clinical grade help (for example "FY2, acute medicine"). A row can be
right in principle and wrong in a detail (a threshold, a time); say which. Rows marked "Clinical lead ruling" can be
challenged too. The open questions behind most rows, with the defaults as first proposed, are in `docs/QUESTIONS.md`
(the codes in brackets, such as A5 or B12, are its question numbers).

Confidence is the proposer's: **high** (standard practice, unlikely to be disputed), **medium** (defensible, but
reasonable clinicians would differ), **low** (a placeholder value or a judgement call that needs a clinician).

Tasks: `hypok-J` / `hypok-I` = `preview-hypok-judgement` / `preview-hypok-instruction`; `stemi-J` / `stemi-I` =
`preview-stemi-judgement` / `preview-stemi-instruction`; `dvt-J` = `preview-dvt-discharge-judgement`. Task files are in
`src/eprbench/data/tasks/`, the bundle in `src/eprbench/data/bundles/preview.json`.
