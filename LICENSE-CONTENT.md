## Fictional data

Every patient, clinician, staff member and record in this repository is fictional. NHS numbers are in the 999 test
range, which is never issued to real patients. The clinical content is a draft and has not been clinically validated
(see `docs/CLINICAL_DECISIONS.md`); it is not for clinical use.
